    Recording settings & help

    Finish overdub keeps the recording and continues playback. Start → Finish is one edit, including every pass. Mute changes playback only; it does not stop recording. The ring shows the shared loop position; layer meters show audio level.

    NAMLNK29 supports a base loop and up to three layers, depending on available memory. Undo restores the last edit; Delete permanently removes a layer. Clearing the base clears all layers. Loops are lost on power-off. 60 s has reduced bandwidth.
